"Should we build our own model?" is almost always the wrong first question.
The right one is: *what's the cheapest path to something we can trust in
production?*

## The default should be "buy"

For 90% of early-stage use cases, a hosted model behind a good retrieval and
evaluation layer wins. You're not in the model business — you're in the
solving-your-customer's-problem business.

## When building (or fine-tuning) earns its keep

Consider going deeper only when at least one is true:

- **Latency or cost** at your scale makes API calls untenable.
- **Privacy or compliance** forbids sending data to a third party.
- **A proprietary data moat** would meaningfully outperform a general model.

Notice none of these is "it would be cool."

## The hidden cost is evaluation, not inference

Whatever you choose, the expensive part is knowing whether it's *working*.
Budget more for evals, guardrails, and monitoring than for the model itself.
That's the part that determines whether you can sleep after you ship.
