A pre-seed founder needed pipeline but couldn't afford a sales hire — and
didn't want to spend 15 hours a week on manual prospect research.

## What we did

- Designed a research step that enriches each lead from public signals and
  drafts a tailored, non-spammy opener the founder reviews before sending.
- Wired the workflow into their existing CRM so nothing gets logged twice.
- Documented the prompts and guardrails so the founder owns and tweaks the
  system without me in the loop.

## Why it worked

The goal was leverage, not autopilot. The founder stays in control of voice and
judgment; AI removes the repetitive research and drafting that was the real
bottleneck.
