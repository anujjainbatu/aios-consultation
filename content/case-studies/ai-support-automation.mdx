The team was drowning. Three support agents were fielding the same dozen
questions every day while real escalations sat in the queue.

## What we did

- Audited 90 days of historical tickets to find the deflectable 70%.
- Built a retrieval-augmented assistant grounded in their help center and
  internal runbooks, with strict citation-or-defer behavior.
- Added a confidence gate so low-certainty answers route to a human with full
  context attached.

## Why it worked

We treated deflection as a routing problem, not a chatbot problem. The model
only answers when it can cite a source — otherwise it hands off cleanly. That
single constraint is what made the team trust it enough to ship.
