A remote team across five time zones was losing hours to asynchronous
"does anyone know..." threads. The knowledge existed — it was just scattered.

## What we did

- Connected Notion, Google Drive, and a curated Slack export into a single
  indexed corpus with per-source access controls.
- Built answers that always link back to the originating document, so people
  trust and verify instead of guessing.
- Added a feedback loop that surfaces stale or contradictory docs to owners.

## Why it worked

The win wasn't the model — it was the grounding and the citations. By making
every answer traceable to a source, the bot became a navigation layer over
existing knowledge rather than a new thing to second-guess.
